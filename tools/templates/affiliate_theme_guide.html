Step 1

Download the theme

Go to affiliatemarketconnect.com/members/domain-web-hosting

  1. Download your done-for-you website.
Step 2

Upload and install the theme

Go to yoursite.com/wp-admin › Appearance › Themes

  1. Click Appearance › Themes.
  2. Click Add Theme.
  1. Click Upload Theme.
  2. Click Choose File and pick affiliate-theme.zip.
  3. Click Install Now.
Step 3

Activate the theme

Go to wp-admin › Appearance › Themes

  1. Point at the Affiliate Theme card.
  2. Click Activate.

What activation creates for you

The moment you activate it, the theme adds seven pages, all as drafts:

  • Landing Page — Centered
  • Landing Page — Split
  • Landing Page — Long Form
  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Service
  • Earnings Disclaimer
  • Affiliate Disclosure

It also adds a new settings page, Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings.

Nothing goes public until you publish it

Step 4

Set your permalinks

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Permalinks

Permalinks set the structure for how each page and post get added to the end of your domain. Set it now, before you publish anything, so no address changes after you save it.

  1. Choose Post name.
  2. Click Save Changes.
Step 5

Create your Resend account and API key

Go to resend.com › API keys

Resend is the email service that sends your automated emails. If you don't have an account you can sign up for free. The theme builds emails, templates and automations in your Resend account, so you don't have to build an email or automation yourself.

  1. Click API keys.
  2. Click Create API key. The Onboarding key is not a full access key.
  1. Type a name you'll recognize.
  2. Choose Full access.
  3. Click Add.
  1. Copy the API key.
  2. Click Done.

Keep your key private

A Full access key controls your whole Resend account. Never paste it into a page, an email or a chat. The theme keeps it on your server and never shows it again: after you save, its field shows only dots.

Step 6

Fill in the theme's settings and save

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

This settings page wires up your whole done-for-you website.

  1. Resend API key - Paste your API key from resend.com.
  2. Sending domain - The domain your emails come from, usually your site's own domain yoursite.com.
  3. From name - The name your subscribers see, 20 characters at most Your Name.
  4. From address - The part before the @.
  5. Reply-to address - Where replies go: leave it empty to use your WordPress admin email address. Optional: read replies in Resend in Step 7.
  6. Mailing address - Your postal mailing address: U.S. law requires one at the bottom of every marketing email, so the emails don't start until it's filled in.
  7. Affiliate link - Paste your full affiliate link. https://affiliatelink.com/yours
  8. Click Save Changes.

What happens when you save

The theme goes to work in your Resend account. Leave the page open and don’t touch anything until it’s done. Waiting time: about 1 minute.

  • The theme adds your domain to your Resend account, and waits until Resend verifies your domain’s DNS records.
  • The theme creates emails, templates, and automations in your Resend account, writing emails in sequences for each of your landing pages.
Step 7

Add your DNS records

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

Add each record where your domain's DNS is managed. Resend may offer to add the records for you. If Resend does not offer to add the records for you, add them manually by copying and pasting.

  1. Scroll down the page to Your sending domain.
  2. Click on the link Open yoursite.com in Resend.
  3. Add all five records Resend needs to verify before you click Check.

The same records in Resend

Go to resend.com › Domains › yoursite.com › Records

  1. Copy and paste each record where your DNS is managed.

Adding the records in Cloudflare

Go to cloudflare.com › Dashboard › Domains › yoursite.com › DNS › Records

  1. Click Add record.
  1. Choose the record's Type.
  2. Paste its Name.
  3. Paste its Content (Target for a CNAME record).
  4. Match its TTL and its Proxy status.
  5. Click Save.
  6. Click Add record again for the next one, until all five are in.
  7. Back in Affiliate Theme Settings, click Check.

Your email sequences

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

When the domain is verified The theme creates your email sequences, and then turns them on.

If Your sending domain still says yoursite.com is waiting for its DNS records.

Give it some time, come back and refresh the page.

StatusWhat it means
LiveThe email sequence is turned on and will deliver its emails to new sign-ups when assigned to a published landing page.
WaitingThe email sequence is ready but still needs something before it can be live. The form on that page stays hidden until then.
Needs updateSomething in Resend is out of date, often because you changed a setting. Click Finish setting up emails. If it says an email has an edit in Resend that is not published, publish or discard that edit in Resend first, then save the settings again.
OffYou turned it off. New sign-ups on that page get no emails and people partway through stop getting emails. If you see It was turned off in Resend., someone switched the automation off in Resend.
Not set upThe emails aren't in Resend yet, or Resend refused them. The line under it gives Resend's reason. Click Finish setting up emails to try again.

Changing your settings later

If you change your From name, Reply-to address, Mailing address, or Affiliate link and click Save Changes, the theme updates all your emails in Resend.

People partway through an email sequence get the new details in their next email as long as the sequence wasn't turned off.

Optional: read replies in Resend

If you want to read replies sent to your email address in Resend, create an inbox.

Skip this if you already get email at your domain's email address like through Google Workspace, Outlook or your host

Configuring your inbox in Resend

Go to resend.com › Domains › yoursite.com › Records

  1. Toggle on Enable receiving.
  2. Add the MX record it shows in Cloudflare.
  3. In Resend, open Inboxes and create an inbox for your From address.

In Affiliate Theme Settings

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

  1. Type your From address in Reply-to address, for example hello@yoursite.com.
  2. Click Save Changes.
Step 8

Edit and publish your landing page

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

  1. Scroll down the page to Your landing pages.
  2. Preview each landing page.
  3. Publish the one you want to use.
  1. Click the form.
  2. In the sidebar that opens on the right, the Form panel holds the settings you can edit:
  3. Button text
  4. Email field label
  5. Name field label
  6. Sequence: each landing page has its own email sequence, written to keep that page's promise.
  7. Click Publish.

Leave the other two landing pages as drafts.

A published page can always be unpublished back to a draft.

The theme writes a description, a social preview and search data for each page

Three things decide what they say:

  • Settings › General. The Site Title and Tagline are what Google, Facebook, X, and LinkedIn show as your site's preview when shared. The Site Icon is the picture that the browser tab shows for your site.
  • Featured image: the picture shown when someone shares your landing page. 1200px × 630px.
  • Excerpt: the description Google shows under your title. Leave it empty to use the first paragraph after the first headline of your page.
Step 9

Make the landing page your home page

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Reading

  1. Choose A static page (select below).
  2. Click the dropdown Homepage.
  3. Choose the landing page you published in Step 8.
  4. Click Save Changes.

Is your landing page missing from the list?

This list shows only published pages. Go back to Step 8 and click Publish.

Step 10

Fill in and publish your legal pages

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

  1. Scroll down the page to Your legal pages.
  2. Edit each legal page.
  3. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER] with your own details.
  4. Click Publish.

If you add analytics, advertising or cookies

Go to wp-admin › Appearance › Editor

Your Privacy Policy says you don't sell or share visitors' information. That stays true only while your site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. Keep each page true to your business. If you start using Google Analytics, any advertising platform or cookies, replace that section of your Privacy Policy:

  1. Click Pages.
  2. Click Privacy Policy.
  3. Remove the heading We Do Not Sell Or Share Your Information and the paragraph after it.
  4. Click the button in the top-left corner +.
  5. Click the tab Patterns.
  6. Click Affiliate Theme.
  7. Click Legal — Privacy: Analytics and Advertising and it will insert itself after the section Who We Share It With.
  8. Replace its placeholder [PROVIDER NAME] with each tool you use.
  9. Click Save.
Step 11

Point WordPress at your Privacy Policy

Go to wp-admin › Settings › Privacy

  1. Click the dropdown list.
  2. Choose the other Privacy Policy.
  3. Click Use This Page.
Finish

Test your funnel

  1. Open a private or incognito browser window and go to your site's address.
  2. Type an email address you own into the form and submit it.
  3. Your browser should land on your affiliate link.
  4. Within a few minutes, the first email arrives in your inbox.
  5. In Resend, click on the Audience tab and the email address you submitted will show as a Contact.

If you sign up again with the same address on the same page, you're still sent to your affiliate link, but no new emails start: each landing page can send its email sequence to the same email address once.

Help

Troubleshooting

WordPress says This theme is already installed.

You're uploading a newer copy over an older one. Click Replace installed with uploaded. Your pages are safe: a theme update never overwrites a landing page or legal page you've edited, nor emails you've edited in Resend.

Under the API key: This key can only send email. The theme needs a key with Full access.

You pasted a key with Sending access, such as Resend's Onboarding key. Make a new key with Full access (Step 5), paste it over the dots and save.

Under the API key: Resend did not accept this key.

The key is wrong or was deleted in Resend. Make a new key with Full access, paste it over the dots and save.

Under the API key: WordPress could not reach Resend. Reload this page in a minute.

Reload the page in a minute. If it keeps happening, ask your host whether they block outgoing connections.

Saving shows The affiliate link is not a usable address. Start it with https://.

Paste the complete link, including the https:// at the start.

Resend says the domain is already in use in another Resend account

A domain can belong to only one Resend account. Use that account's API key here, or a different domain.

My DNS records still aren't verified

DNS changes can take a few hours to propagate. Check each record's name and value against Your sending domain, look for a name with your domain added twice, and make sure the CNAME records are DNS only on Cloudflare. Then click Check once more. You can also check www.whatsmydns.net.

My landing page says Opt-in form is not ready:

Only administrators see this note. Visitors see no form at all until it's fixed.

My forms vanished and every email sequence says Not set up

Open Affiliate Theme Settings and click Finish setting up emails: the theme finds them again without making new ones.

An email sequence says Needs update, or It was turned off in Resend.

If you switched off an email sequence in Resend, the theme will turn it off too; click Turn on.

Your email sequences say Open tracking is off for this domain.

Your emails still send. To turn it on, see Configuring your domain in Resend in Step 7.

Your email sequences say Click tracking is off for this domain.

Your emails still send. To turn it on, see Configuring your domain in Resend in Step 7.

Visitors see Service unavailable. Please try again shortly.

Your server got no answer from Resend. It's usually brief. If it lasts, check that the line under your API key reads Resend accepted this key.

Visitors see Something went wrong. Please try again.

Resend refused the sign-up, or the page's email sequence is turned off.

Emails stopped arriving

You may have reached your Resend plan's daily or monthly limit. Check your usage in Resend, under Metrics.

A legal page isn't in my footer

Only published pages are linked. Open the page and click Publish. The link then appears on every page at once.

How do I change the copyright line in the footer?

Go to Appearance › Editor › Patterns › Footer, edit the line © 2026 — All rights reserved. then save.

The legal pages that come with this theme are a starting template, not legal advice. Review them for your own business, or have a professional review them.
