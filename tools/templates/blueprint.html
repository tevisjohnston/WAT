The foundation

How Traffic Becomes Profit

  • Traffic costs money.
  • Traffic makes money.
  • It takes money to make money.

That is not just a catchy phrase. It is the basic truth of online business, and it separates the people who succeed from the people who spin their wheels.

The quickest way to make money with affiliate marketing is to send targeted traffic to an offer. When somebody shows interest in a specific kind of product, by joining an email list or by buying something, they become traffic you can target.

Your mission

Build a website that is worth sending traffic to.

A website worth sending traffic to is one that gives somebody else real value. When you send targeted traffic to a website like that, you can turn that value into income.

The rest of this blueprint shows you how, in four steps.

Step 1

Product Selection

MarketplaceWarriorPlus

Your first decision shapes everything that follows. Choose a product you genuinely believe will help the person who buys it. Look for a no-brainer: a product that gives so much value that buying it is an easy yes.

Then check the numbers that show its customers are happy. Your reputation depends on the quality of what you promote.

1

High Conversion Rate

More of your visitors become buyers, so every click you pay for works harder.

2

Low Refund Rate

Happy customers keep what they bought, and you keep your commission.

3

Good Customer Value

Real value builds your reputation, and satisfied buyers come back.

4

High Commission %

A bigger share of every sale means you earn more, faster.

Where to find these numbers

WarriorPlus shows stats such as conversion rate, refund rate and earnings per click (EPC) for the offers in its marketplace. Compare a few products in your niche before you commit to one. Many launches there pay 50% to 100% commission on the front-end product.

Step 2

Domain + Web Hosting

Your domain and web hosting are the foundation of your online business. Choose a domain that relates to the product you promote. It earns instant trust from visitors who know nothing about you, and a visitor only clicks a link or a button on a website they trust.

Trust = Sales

Choose a .com domain whenever you can. Domains start at around $10 a year, but your website also needs web hosting to be live on the internet. Most hosting companies sell both, and many hosting plans include a free domain for the first year.

Step 3

Website Creation

Owning the website your business runs on gives you complete control. This is where you build your affiliate marketing business, in five parts.

  1. Landing Page

    Create one page for all of your visitors to land on. It needs:

    1. A title
    2. A subtitle
    3. An image
    4. A form that collects {name} and {email}
    5. A Submit button that sends them to your Thank You Page

    Focus on clear, compelling copy that speaks to your visitor’s needs.

  2. Lead Magnet

    Offer something valuable for free:

    1. An eBook you write yourself (ChatGPT can help with the first draft)
    2. A PLR product (Private Label Rights)

    This is what you trade for an email address.

  3. Email Sequence

    Write compelling emails that keep promoting your offers. The first email goes out automatically as soon as a visitor submits their {name} and {email} on your landing page in exchange for your lead magnet. The rest go out automatically on a set schedule.

  4. Autoresponder

    Connect your form to an email service:

    1. AWeber
    2. GetResponse

    It sends your emails automatically over days and weeks, so your offers keep reaching your subscribers.

  5. Thank You Page

    This is where a visitor lands after subscribing. Give it a prominent button that sends subscribers to your affiliate link. This is where conversions happen.

The funnel flow

1A visitor arrives at your landing page
2They subscribe for your lead magnet
3They land on your Thank You Page
4Your automated emails keep following up
5They click your affiliate link, and you earn a commission

Not all traffic is created equal. You want people who are already warmed up and interested in solutions like the one you promote.

Why your list is the secret

The clicks you buy in a campaign only bring in new subscribers. The sales come from your whole list, and everybody who stays subscribed can buy again in the next campaign. That is why the roadmap counts sales against your total subscribers, not just the new ones.
