Step 1

Download the theme

Go to affiliatemarketconnect.com/members/domain-web-hosting

Download your DFY website affiliate-theme.zip.

Keep it zipped. WordPress installs the .zip file exactly as you downloaded it. Don't open it, rename it, or change what's inside.

On a Mac, Safari may unzip it for you

If your Downloads folder shows a folder called affiliate-theme instead of affiliate-theme.zip, right-click the folder and choose Compress “affiliate-theme”. That makes a new affiliate-theme.zip you can upload. To stop Safari doing this, turn off Safari › Settings › General › Open “safe” files after downloading.

Step 2

Upload and install the theme

Go to yoursite.com/wp-admin

  1. In the left menu, click Appearance. The Themes screen opens.
  2. Click Add Theme at the top.
  1. Click Upload Theme next to the Add Themes title.
  2. Click Choose File and pick affiliate-theme.zip.
  3. Click Install Now. After a few seconds WordPress says Theme installed successfully.
Step 3

Activate the theme

Go to Appearance › Themes

Click Activate or point at the Affiliate Theme card and click Activate.

What activation creates for you

The moment you activate it, the theme adds seven pages, all as drafts:

  • Three landing pages, the same offer in three layouts: Landing Page — Centered, Landing Page — Split and Landing Page — Long Form. You'll use one.
  • Four legal pages: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Earnings Disclaimer and Affiliate Disclosure.

It also adds a new screen, Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings, with three sections, Resend, Sender and Funnel. You fill it in during Step 6.

Nothing goes public until you publish it

The theme creates every page as a draft. You can take your time with each step. Visitors see a page only after you click Publish.

Step 4

Set your permalinks

Go to Settings › Permalinks

Permalinks are your pages' web addresses. Post name gives each page a short, readable one, such as yoursite.com/privacy/. Set it now, before you publish anything, so no address changes after you share it.

  1. Under Permalink structure, choose Post name. The Custom Structure box below it changes to /%postname%/.
  2. Click Save Changes at the bottom of the screen.
Step 5

Create your Resend account and API key

Go to resend.com

Resend is the email service that sends your welcome emails. The theme sets everything up in your own Resend account, so you never have to build an email in Resend yourself. It only needs a key that lets it do that.

Go to resend.com and sign up for a free account. Then make the key:

  1. In the left menu, click API keys.
  2. Click Create API key. The Onboarding key Resend made for you can only send email, so the theme can't use it.
  1. Under Name, type a name you'll recognize, such as your site's address.
  2. Under Permission, choose Full access. This matters: the theme adds your domain and your emails to Resend, and a key with Sending access can't do that. Leave Domain at All domains.
  3. Click Add.
  1. Click the copy button at the end of the key. Resend shows the key only once, so paste it straight into the theme's settings (Step 6), or somewhere safe until then.
  2. Click Done.

Keep your key private

A Full access key controls your whole Resend account. Never paste it into a page, an email or a chat. The theme keeps it on your server and never shows it again: after you save, its field shows only dots.

Resend's free plan

At the time of writing, Resend's free plan sends 100 emails a day and 3,000 a month, and holds 1,000 contacts. Check the current numbers on Resend's pricing page (resend.com/pricing).

  • Each new subscriber gets 6 emails over 28 days from the Centered and Long Form pages, or 5 emails over 4 days from the Split page.
  • Every subscriber stays a contact in Resend after their emails end, and counts toward the contact limit.
  • Starting on the free plan is fine. The theme doesn't show your usage, so check it in Resend, under Metrics, once you start sending traffic.
  • When you outgrow it: at the time of writing, Transactional Pro ($20 a month) sends 50,000 emails a month with no daily limit, but keeps 1,000 contacts. Marketing Pro ($40 a month) raises contacts to 5,000, and higher tiers go further.
Step 6

Fill in the theme's settings and save

Go to Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

This one screen wires up your whole funnel. Fill it in from top to bottom. The numbers match the screenshot.

  1. Resend API key. Paste the key from Step 5. After you save, the line under it reads Resend accepted this key.
  2. Sending domain. The domain your emails come from, usually your site's own domain, typed like yoursite.com (no https://, no www.). Saving adds it to your Resend account. If it's already in your Resend account, the theme uses it as it is.
  3. From name. The name your subscribers see, 20 characters at most. Leave it empty to use your Site Title.
  4. From address. The part before the @. hello is filled in for you, so your emails come from hello@yoursite.com.
  5. Reply-to address. Where replies go: an inbox you read. Leave it empty to use your WordPress administration email address. To read replies in Resend instead, see Optional: read replies in Resend in Step 7.
  6. Mailing address. Your postal address. Every email shows it at the bottom, and no email goes out without it.
  7. Affiliate link. Paste your full affiliate link, starting with https://. The form and your emails send each new subscriber here.
  8. Click Save Changes.

What happens when you save

The theme goes to work in your Resend account. Leave the page open and don’t touch anything until it’s done. Waiting time: about 1 minute.

  • The theme adds your domain to your Resend account, and waits until your domain’s DNS records are verified.
  • The theme writes your emails for each landing page, and creates templates and automations in your Resend account.
Step 7

Add your DNS records

Go to Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

Resend needs to verify your domain before it sends any emails from it. Scroll down the page to Your sending domain where the table lists each record Resend asks you to add.

Add all five before you click Check.

  1. Add each record where your domain's DNS is managed (at Cloudflare, or at the company you bought the domain from). Resend may offer to add the records for you on its own page for your domain. If Resend does not offer to add the records for you, add them manually by copying and pasting.
  2. Click Check. Clicking check forces Resend to check the records and verify the domain by itself, usually within a few minutes, sometimes a few hours. Clicking Check again later if the table is still showing missing records will restart Resend's verification process.

The same records in Resend

Go to resend.com › Domains › yoursite.com › Records

Type, Name, Content, TTL and, for the MX record, Priority. Copy each value into the same field where your DNS is managed. Where Resend's Proxy status column reads DNS Only, set that record's proxy the same way on Cloudflare (see Adding the records on Cloudflare below).

  1. Your domain in Domains.
  2. Its Records tab.

Adding the records on Cloudflare

Go to cloudflare.com › Dashboard › Domains › yoursite.com › DNS › Records

  1. On your Cloudflare home page, click your domain.
  1. In the left menu, open DNS and click Records.
  2. Click Add record.
  1. Choose the record's Type, paste its Name, Content, TTL, Name, and Proxy status.
  1. For the two records that have Tpye: CNAME toggle the Proxy status off, so it reads DNS only with a grey cloud. A proxied (orange) record won't be verified.
  2. Click Save. Then click Add record for the next one, until all five are in.

Then click Check

Back in Affiliate Theme Settings, click Check. Resend checks your records and verifies the domain, usually within a few minutes. Until it does, your email sequences wait.

When the domain is verified

The theme sees that the domain is verified and turns your email sequences on, and Your email sequences shows each one as Live.

If Your sending domain still says yoursite.com is waiting for its DNS records, give it some time and refresh the page.

Configuring your domain in Resend

Go to Domains › yoursite.com › Configuration

  1. In the Configuration tab.
  2. Under Enable tracking metrics, click Configure.
  3. Tick Enable click tracking.
  4. Tick Enable open tracking.
  5. Type links as the Subdomain, and go on to the next screen.
  6. Add the CNAME record it shows. Click Auto configure if Resend offers it, or add the record manually where your DNS is managed. Then click I've added the record.
  1. Configuration tab
  2. The tracking subdomain and its options.
  3. The record to add.
  4. Wait until the new record is verified..

Optional: read replies in Resend

Create an inbox in Resend to read replies sent to your email address. When a subscriber replies to one of your emails, the reply goes to your Reply-to address.

Skip this if you already get email at your domain's email address like through Google Workspace, Outlook or your host

  1. In Resend, open your domain's Records tab and switch Enable receiving on.
  2. Add the MX record it shows in Cloudflare.
  3. In Resend, open Inboxes and create an inbox for your From address, such as hello@yoursite.com.
  4. In Affiliate Theme Settings, set Reply-to address to the same address as your From address and click Save Changes. The theme updates all your emails.

Check your Inboxes for replies.

Step 8

Edit and publish your landing page

Go to Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

Publish only the landing page you use

Preview each landing page and publish the one you want to use. Leave the other two landing pages as drafts. A published page can always be unpublished back to a draft.

Every headline, paragraph, list and image can be edited: click it and type, or replace the image. The opt-in form can also be edited through the sidebar:

  1. Click the form.

The sidebar that opens on the right has these settings you can edit:

  1. Button text
  2. Email field label
  3. Name field label
  4. Sequence: each landing page has its own email sequence, written to keep that page's promise.
  5. Click Publish.

How your page looks in Google and when it's shared

The theme writes a description, a social preview and search data for each page. Three things decide what they say:

  • Settings › General. The Site Title and Tagline are what Google, Facebook, X, and LinkedIn show as your site's preview when shared. The Site Icon is the picture that the browser tab shows for your site.
  • Featured image: the picture shown when someone shares your landing page. 1200px × 630px.
  • Excerpt: the description Google shows under your title. Leave it empty to use the first paragraph after the first headline of your page.
Step 9

Make the landing page your home page

Go to Settings › Reading

  1. Under Your homepage displays, choose A static page (select below).
  2. In the Homepage list, choose the landing page you published in Step 8.
  3. Click Save Changes.

Now visit your site's address. Your landing page is the first thing people see.

Is your landing page missing from the list?

This list shows only published pages. Go back to Step 8 and click Publish.

Step 10

Fill in and publish your legal pages

Go to Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

Each legal page is written in plain English and uses square-bracket placeholders wherever your own details belong. Replace every one, on all four pages, before you publish: a published page joins your site's footer. Draft pages are never linked. Each placeholder is spelled the same way everywhere, so your browser's find command (Ctrl + F, or Cmd + F on a Mac) finds them all.

PlaceholderReplace it with
[Your Business Name]Your name or your business's legal name
[yoursite.com]Your domain, for example mysite.com
[you@example.com]An email address where visitors can reach you
[Your Mailing Address]Your postal address, the same one as in your emails
[Your State/Country]The state or country whose law applies to your business
[Month Day, Year]The date the page takes effect, usually the day you publish it

Keep each page true to your business. These pages are a starting template, not legal advice.

If you add analytics, advertising or cookies

Your Privacy Policy says you don't sell or share visitors' information. That stays true only while your site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. If you start using Google Analytics, any advertising platform or cookies, replace that section of your Privacy Policy:

  1. Go to yoursite.com/wp-admin
  2. Go to Appearance › Editor
  3. Pages
  4. Open your Privacy Policy page in the editor.
  5. Remove the We Do Not Sell Or Share Your Information heading and the paragraph after it.
  6. Click the + button in the top-left corner.
  7. Switch from the Blocks tab to the Patterns tab.
  8. Choose Affiliate Theme.
  9. Choose Legal — Privacy: Analytics and Advertising and it will insert itself after Who We Share It With. Replace its bracket [Provider Name] with each tool you use like for example Google Analytics. Then click Save.
Step 11

Point WordPress at your Privacy Policy

Go to Settings › Privacy

  1. Open the Change your Privacy Policy page list. Privacy Policy appears twice. The one with the checkmark is WordPress's own unpublished draft, so choose the other Privacy Policy.
  2. Click Use This Page.
Your emails

Your email sequences

Go to Settings › Affiliate Theme Settings

Each landing page starts its own sequence of emails.

StatusWhat it means
LiveNew sign-ups on that page get its emails.
WaitingThe email sequence is ready but still needs something before it can be live. The form on that page stays hidden until then.
Needs updateSomething in Resend is out of date, often because you changed a setting. Click Finish setting up emails. If it says an email has an edit in Resend that is not published, publish or discard that edit in Resend first, then save the settings again.
OffYou turned it off. New sign-ups on that page get no emails. If you see It was turned off in Resend., someone switched the automation off in Resend.
Not set upThe emails aren't in Resend yet, or Resend refused them. The line under it gives Resend's reason. Click Finish setting up emails to try again.

The buttons on each row

  • Turn off stops an email sequence. New sign-ups on that page get no emails, and people partway through stop getting emails. Turn on starts it again for new sign-ups, but doesn't restart anyone who was stopped.
  • Reset these emails to the theme's copy replaces any edits you made to the emails in Resend and puts back the original copy.
  • Open in Resend opens the email sequence's automation at resend.com.

Changing your settings later

The theme will update all your emails in Resend if there are any changes that are made to your affiliate link, mailing address, From name or Reply-to address when you click Save Changes. People partway through an email sequence get the new details in their next email as long as the sequence wasn't turned off.

Finish

Test your funnel

  1. Open a private or incognito browser window and go to your site's address.
  2. Type an email address you own into the form and submit it.
  3. Your browser should land on your affiliate link.
  4. Within a few minutes, the first email arrives in your inbox.
  5. In Resend, open Audience and the email address you submitted will show as a Contact.

If you sign up again with the same address on the same page, you're still sent to your affiliate link, but no new emails start: each email address can get each page's email sequence once: you can get each page's email sequence at the same time. The same address on a different landing page starts that page's email sequence.

Help

Troubleshooting

Uploading the theme shows The theme is missing the style.css stylesheet.

You uploaded a different zip, or one whose contents were changed. Download affiliate-theme.zip again and upload it exactly as downloaded (see the Mac note in Step 1).

Uploading shows The link you followed has expired.

Reload the Add Themes page and upload again. If it keeps happening, your host's upload limit is too small. The theme is under 2 MB, so ask your host to allow uploads of at least 2 MB.

WordPress says This theme is already installed.

You're uploading a newer copy over an older one. Click Replace installed with uploaded. Your pages are safe: a theme update never overwrites a landing page or legal page you've edited, nor emails you've edited in Resend.

Under the API key: This key can only send email. The theme needs a key with Full access.

You pasted a key with Sending access, such as Resend's Onboarding key. Make a new key with Full access (Step 5), paste it over the dots and save.

Under the API key: Resend did not accept this key.

The key is wrong or was deleted in Resend. Make a new key with Full access, paste it over the dots and save.

Under the API key: WordPress could not reach Resend. Reload this page in a minute.

Your server couldn't reach Resend just then, or Resend was busy. It says nothing about your key. Reload the page in a minute. If it keeps happening, ask your host whether they block outgoing connections.

Saving shows That is not a Resend API key. A Resend API key starts with re_.

The text you pasted isn't a key. Copy it again from Resend; if you no longer have it, make a new one. The key you had before, if any, is kept.

Saving shows The affiliate redirect URL is not a usable address. Start it with https://.

Paste the complete link, including the https:// at the start.

Resend says the domain is already in use in another Resend account

A domain can belong to only one Resend account. Use that account's API key here, or a different domain.

My DNS records still aren't verified

DNS changes can take a few hours to propagate. Check each record's name and value against Your sending domain, look for a name with your domain added twice (see Step 7), and make sure the CNAME records are DNS only on Cloudflare. Then click Check once more. You can also check www.whatsmydns.net.

Resend says Partially Verified

One record, often the links one, isn't verified yet, and Resend can't send until it is. Add the missing record or fix it.

My landing page says Opt-in form is not ready:

Only administrators see this note. Visitors see no form at all until it's fixed.

My forms vanished and every email sequence says Not set up

Open Affiliate Theme Settings and click Finish setting up emails: the theme finds them again without making new ones.

An email sequence says Needs update, or It was turned off in Resend.

The theme will turn off an email sequence you or someone else switched off in Resend; click Turn on.

Open tracking is off for this domain.

Your emails still send. See If your domain was already in Resend in Step 7.

Click tracking is off for this domain.

Your emails still send. See If your domain was already in Resend in Step 7.

Visitors see Please try again in a moment. or Please try again in a few minutes.

The first means Resend was busy for a second. The second means more than five sign-ups came from one internet address within ten minutes, which stops someone filling your list with fake addresses.

Visitors see Service unavailable. Please try again shortly.

Your server got no answer from Resend. It's usually brief. If it lasts, check that the line under your API key reads Resend accepted this key.

Visitors see Something went wrong. Please try again.

Resend refused the sign-up, or the page's email sequence is turned off.

Emails stopped arriving

You may have reached your Resend plan's daily or monthly limit. Check your usage in Resend, under Metrics.

A legal page isn't in my footer

Only published pages are linked. Open the page and click Publish. The link then appears on every page at once.

How do I change the copyright line in the footer?

Go to Appearance › Editor › Patterns › Footer, open the footer, click the © 2026 — All rights reserved. line and type your own. Then save.

The legal pages that come with this theme are a starting template, not legal advice. Review them for your own business, or have a professional review them.
